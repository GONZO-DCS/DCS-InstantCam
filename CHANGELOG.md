# Changelog

## v1.4.2
- Installer: optional desktop shortcut (tick box on the Components page)

## v1.4.0
- Tray menu: photos folder, caption editor, your note, quick settings, photo resolution, start with Windows
- Edit the handwritten caption after the mission (preview as you type); the caption is stored inside each PNG
- Sharper photos: 1536 px by default (1024/2048 selectable), bicubic sampling and anti-aliasing
- Place names now come from the map's airfields (works on packed maps like Syria)
- Long caption lines shrink to fit instead of being cut off; proper aircraft names (F/A-18C Hornet)
- Settings changes apply without restarting DCS

## v1.3.0
- Flat screen: the camera now follows your aim, held up in front of the target (`FollowAim=0` for the old corner layout)
- Handwritten captions: nearest town, mission date and time, pilot, aircraft and your own note
- Instant-film look: rounded film-mask vignette, soft edges, faded colour, pink highlights, grain
- Worn paper card; the print you watch develop is the same card that gets saved

## v1.2.0
- Flat-screen (non-VR) support

## v1.1.0
- New 90s/2000s-style camera body, film counter and 10-shot film packs

## v1.0.0
- First version: VR handheld camera, mouse aim, zoom, developing print, captions
