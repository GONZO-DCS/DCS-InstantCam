**DCS Instant Cam v1.3.0** – a handheld instant camera for every aircraft in DCS World, in VR and on a flat screen.

### New in 1.3
- Flat screen: the camera follows your aim, held up in front of the target
- Handwritten captions: nearest town, mission date and time, pilot, aircraft and your own note
- Instant-film look and worn paper card

### Install
Download **DCS_InstantCam_Setup_v1.3.exe** below and run it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed).
In the cockpit, press **Mouse 4** to raise the camera.
